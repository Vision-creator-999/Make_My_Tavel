Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026. How we manage and secure your personal data.

Important Notice / Disclaimer

This document is a placeholder template for demonstration purposes. Before deploying this website live to production or using it for a real-world company, please consult a qualified legal professional to review and modify this privacy policy to ensure compliance with privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, IT Act 2000).

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to deliver efficient, secure travel bookings on our platform. The types of data include:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, and billing parameters provided during signup, profile edits, or reservation requests.
  • Credentials & Session Data: Passwords (encrypted using bcrypt hash algorithms) and active authentication token codes.
  • Booking Details: Records of hotels, cab rides, and holiday packages reserved, including amounts, check-in dates, and payment states.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed to manage your account and secure your bookings. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Verify your account credentials during login or third-party OAuth access.
  • Record and confirm reservation requests, update stay inventory, and generate booking invoices.
  • Manage loyalty point balances and check promo code locks (e.g. tracking your cab bookings counts or invitations).
  • Submit newsletter subscription lists and contact form requests.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local browser storage and secure cookie scripts to keep you logged in between sessions, track active promo values, and remember your display preferences. You can disable cookies inside your browser settings, but doing so may prevent access to parts of the booking and profile dashboards.

4. Third-Party Services

We share data with verified third-party integrations to manage essential application actions:

  • Google Sign-In: We use Google OAuth to securely authenticate you without sharing password credentials. Google's privacy policy regulates their authorization flow.
  • Cloudinary: Profile pictures and verification document files are uploaded to and optimized by Cloudinary cloud database systems.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Customer data is stored in secure, encrypted cloud databases managed by MongoDB.

5. Your Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You have the right to edit your profile parameters (name, phone number, avatar) at any time. You can also request complete account deletion and deletion of your booking history by contacting our support team.

6. Contact for Privacy Concerns

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, our data collection practices, or wish to report a security concern, please contact our data protection officer at:

privacy@makemytravel.com
