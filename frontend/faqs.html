General FAQs

Got questions about accounts, loyalty programs, or payment security? We have answers.

How do I create an account?

Creating an account is simple. Click on the Login / Sign Up button in the top right corner. You can either sign up using your email and a password or click on the Sign in with Google button to register instantly using your Google credentials.

Is Google Sign-In secure?

Yes. We use standard OAuth 2.0 validation via official Google API libraries. We only request basic public profile metadata (name, email address, profile picture URL) to verify your account identity. We never see or store your Google password.

How do I earn and redeem loyalty points?

For every hotel, cab, or holiday package booking you complete, you earn loyalty points (1 point per ₹100 spent). You can check your balance in your My Profile dashboard. You can redeem these points as a discount on subsequent reservations at checkout.

How do I unlock locked promo codes?

Some premium promo codes have specific unlock criteria to prevent misuse:

- CAR15: Locked until you invite/refer at least 1 friend from your profile page.

- TRAVEL30: Locked until you book at least 1 verified cab ride on our platform.

Once you meet these parameters, the codes unlock automatically and can be applied at checkout.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, AMEX), Netbanking channels from all leading banks, and secure instant UPI payment links. All card data is parsed through PCI-DSS compliant secure vaults.

Can I list multiple hotels or cabs under one account?

Yes. If you register as a partner, you can add multiple listings (e.g., a fleet of cabs or multiple hotels) using your partner dashboard. Each listing can be updated with custom rates, specific cities, and room layouts individually.

How do I update my profile details?

Go to the My Profile page. You can edit your phone number, display name, and upload a new profile picture. Profile pictures are securely processed and optimized using Cloudinary media services.

Why did my payment fail?

Payment failures typically occur due to temporary bank network timeouts, insufficient funds, or incorrect security OTP entry. If money is debited from your account during a failed booking attempt, our automated reconciliation program restores the debited amount to your bank source within 24 hours.

How can I check the status of my bookings?

Log in and navigate to the My Profile dashboard. The "My Bookings" section lists all transactions chronologically with their current status: pending (waiting for partner confirmation), confirmed (reservation secured), completed (trip completed/checked out), or cancelled.
