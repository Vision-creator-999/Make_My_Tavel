Help Center

Find quick answers to common issues regarding bookings, cabs, hotels, and refunds.

Cancellations & Modifications

How do I cancel a booking?

To cancel an active reservation, follow these steps:

  • Navigate to your My Profile dashboard after logging in.
  • Look for the booking record under your active Bookings List.
  • If the booking status is listed as confirmed, a red Cancel button will be visible. Click the button and confirm your action.
  • Once submitted, the system will instantly process the cancellation, update the database status to cancelled, and restore room/cab availability dynamically.
Can I cancel a booking if its status is 'pending'?

According to our platform rules, only bookings with a status of confirmed can be cancelled using the self-service cancellation option. If your booking is still pending confirmation or has already completed, the cancellation option will be disabled. Please contact support if you need manual assistance with pending status adjustments.

Stay & Drive Specifics

How does the hotel room replenishment system behave during cancellations?

When you cancel a confirmed hotel booking, our database engine automatically triggers a inventory restore operation. The exact number of rooms reserved in your transaction are immediately re-added back to the hotel's room count (`availableRooms += rooms`). This ensures real-time accurate availability for other travelers searching the platform.

How do I contact my driver for cab bookings?

Once a cab booking status turns to confirmed, the details of your assigned driver and vehicle are displayed on your profile page under the active bookings section. A phone icon will be provided to get direct voice contact with the driver. Additionally, you will receive driver details via SMS 30 minutes before your scheduled pickup time.

Refunds & Partner Listings

How do refunds work on cancelled transactions?

When a confirmed booking is cancelled, the database immediately checks its payment state:

  • If the payment status is listed as paid, our gateway system changes the status to refunded.
  • The refund transaction is immediately initialized and credited back to the original payment source (credit card, bank account, or UPI handle).
  • Credits are typically processed in 5-7 business days depending on banking channels.
How do I list my hotel, cab, or holiday package on MakeMyTravel?

Partners are welcome! To register your asset:

  • Click the **Partner with Us** button in the header menu or the footer.
  • Select the specific asset type: **List your Cab**, **List your Hotel**, or **List your Package**.
  • Fill out the registration details (credentials, location, pricing, availability parameters) and submit. Our backend panel will review your listing and list it live on approval.
